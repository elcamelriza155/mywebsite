Mom Life and Parenting

Balancing School and Motherhood

  • Use a simple daily plan: choose your top 3 school tasks for the day and focus on those first.
  • Study in small blocks (20–30 minutes). Short sessions can be easier when you have a child to care for.
  • Create a weekly schedule that includes study time, family time, rest, and chores—everything matters.
  • Ask for support when you can (partner, family, friend, classmate). You do not need to do everything alone.
  • Be kind to yourself. Progress is progress, even if it’s slow.

Budget-Friendly Tips

  • Track your spending for one week to see where your money goes.
  • Plan simple meals. Cooking at home can save money and time, especially with a short weekly meal plan.
  • Prepare school supplies early and buy only what you need. Look for sales or second-hand options.
  • Use free resources: online notes, library books, and free learning websites.
  • Set a small “emergency” amount every week, even if it’s just a little.
