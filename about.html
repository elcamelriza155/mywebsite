About me

Hi! I’m Riza.

I am a student and a mom. Balancing school and motherhood is not always easy, but it gives me strength and motivation everyday.

I created this website to share my journey as a mommy student. Being a mother while studying has taught me patience, hardwork, and determination. Every challenge I face reminds me why I continue working hard, for my son and for our future.

I hope my story can inspire other mothers to continue their dreams, no matter how difficult the journey may be.
